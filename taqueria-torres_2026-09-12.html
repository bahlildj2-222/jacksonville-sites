Our Story

Late-night street tacos, gorditas, and tortas from Jacksonville's orange food trailer.

An authentic Mexican food trailer established in 2002, Taqueria Torres has fed Jacksonville for over two decades — building a loyal local following for fresh-to-order street tacos, gorditas, tortas, and specialty Mexican burgers under its bright orange awning.
