Dimensional Layer Breakdown

Explore six common roof components. This educational illustration is not a specification or promise about the contractor’s products.

Layer 01

Structural Roof Decking

Roof decking provides a structural surface beneath the roof covering. Material and thickness depend on the building and project requirements.

MaterialProject-dependent decking
RatingStructural Load
Layer 02

Ice & Water Leak Barrier

Self-sealing elastomeric membrane installed along eaves, valleys, chimneys, and roof penetrations to prevent water backup from ice dams or wind-driven rain.

AdhesionSelf-Sealing Polymer
ProtectionEaves & Valleys
Layer 03

Synthetic Underlayment

Underlayment sits beneath the roof covering as an additional protective layer. Performance varies by product and installation.

Tear StrengthProduct-dependent
BreathabilityProduct-dependent
Layer 04

Drip Edge & Starter Strip

Corrosion-resistant metal drip edge flashing along eaves and rakes, bonded with factory-applied adhesive starter shingles to resist wind uplift.

FlashingHeavy Gauge Steel
Wind LockStarter Adhesive
Layer 05

Architectural Shingles

Architectural shingles form the visible roof covering. Color, material, impact rating and warranty depend on the selected product.

SystemProduct-dependent
WarrantyLocal Broken Arrow
Layer 06

Ridge Vent Airflow System

Continuous ridge venting installed along the roof peak to balance attic thermal temperatures, exhaust heat, and prevent attic moisture buildup.

AirflowContinuous Net Free Area
ThermalAttic Balance
