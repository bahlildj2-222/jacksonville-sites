Our Story

Where the town has breakfast

Super Clean Laundromat is a top-rated, family-owned independent laundromat in South Tulsa known for its immaculate cleanliness, friendly staff, and well-maintained equipment. It offers versatile washer and dryer sizes to accommodate everyday laundry as well as heavy comforters and bedding. Local residents frequently praise its safe, welcoming environment and efficient self-service and drop-off wash-and-fold offerings.

Machines & Services

What makes it easy

Self-Service Washers & Dryers
High-efficiency commercial washing machines and dryers available in multiple load capacities ranging from standard single loads to extra-large multi-load units.
Wash, Dry, & Fold Service
Convenient drop-off laundry service where staff washes, dries, and neatly folds garments, linens, and everyday wear for busy customers.
Oversized Bedspread & Comforter Cleaning
Heavy-duty large-capacity washers designed specifically for bulky items including bedspreads, duvets, comforters, sleeping bags, and small rugs.
Free Wi-Fi & Lounge Seating
Clean, comfortable waiting lounge with complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi access, tables, and seating while waiting for laundry cycles to finish.
Vending & Laundry Supplies
On-site vending machines offering laundry detergent, fabric softeners, bleach, travel bags, plus cold beverages and snacks.
Dedicated On-Site Parking
Spacious and accessible front parking lot allowing easy unloading and loading of heavy laundry bags directly at the entrance.
