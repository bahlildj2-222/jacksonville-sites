Our Story

The Campbell Hotel is a famous 1927 historic boutique hotel situated on Route 66 near Downtown Tulsa and the University of Tulsa.

The Campbell Hotel is a famous 1927 historic boutique hotel situated on Route 66 near Downtown Tulsa and the University of Tulsa. Originally built as the Max Campbell Building, it is renowned for its 26 custom-themed luxury suites, rich architectural preservation, on-site spa and lounge, and proximity to Mother Road Market. Guests value its intimate, eclectic charm and personalized local hospitality.
