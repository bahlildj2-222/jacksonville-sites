Our Story

A studio built on color

The Glossary is a design concept for a boutique nail studio — a love letter to color, craft and the small rituals of a great manicure. It shows what a nail studio's website could feel like: warm, personal and unmistakably its own.

Inside the concept, every service is painted by hand, every guest leaves with color they chose, and the wall of shades doubles as the studio's library. Call the studio for its current services, availability and hours.
