Our Story

Founded and run by local pitmaster Richard Kincade, Flamin Bacon gained fame as the birthplace of the bacon-wrapped turkey. This distinct Jacksonville smokehouse serves small-batch Texas barbecue, smoked meats, and custom bacon creations — and when it's gone, it's gone.
